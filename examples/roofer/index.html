Before you ring.

Do I need a repair or a new roof?

It depends on the age of the roof and how widespread the damage is. We inspect, show you photos, and tell you honestly which it is. If a repair will do, we say so.

What do I do while I wait for you?

Stay off the roof. If water is coming in, put a bucket under it and move anything valuable. Take a photo from the ground if it is safe. Ring us and we will tell you what to do next.

Do you work with insurers?

We can give you a written report and photos to send to your insurer. Whether a claim is paid is up to them.

What if you find more wrong once we start?

We stop, show you, and agree the extra cost in writing before we carry on.

Do you do small jobs?

Yes. A few slipped tiles is as welcome as a full re-roof.
