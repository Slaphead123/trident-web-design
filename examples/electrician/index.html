Common questions

My circuit keeps tripping. What should I do?

Unplug anything on that circuit, reset it once, and if it trips again leave it off and ring us. Do not keep resetting it.

How often do I need a safety report?

Landlords need one at least every five years. Homeowners often get one when buying or when a home is over ten years old.

Do you give certificates?

Yes, for notifiable work. On a real site the owner states which certificates they supply.

Do you do emergency work?

Yes. Ring and we will tell you how soon we can be there.
