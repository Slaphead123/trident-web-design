Patch tests

Lash and brow tints need a patch test at least 48 hours before your first appointment. Ring or message and we will book one in.

Looking after your nails

Wear gloves for washing up, use cuticle oil each day, and do not pick at gel or acrylic. If one lifts, get in touch and we will sort it.

Running late?

Tell us as early as you can. We will find another slot where we can.
