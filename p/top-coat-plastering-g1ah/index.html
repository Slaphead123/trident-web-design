What Richard plasters.

  • Interiors

    Smooth skim finishing coat over old walls and ceilings is a quick fix for most tired rooms. Cracked walls and blown plaster can be remedied.

  • Exteriors and rendering

    Usually a traditional mix of sand, cement, lime and waterproof additive. Old cracked render can often lead to damp issues in your property, so it is a good place to look if you have such issues. New render also adds a pleasing look to your home, with different finishes, textures and even colours.

  • Plastering repairs

    A wall or ceiling re-plastered, coving, cornice work, plaster arches and mouldings, dry-lining or plastering over Artex.

  • Building services

    Blocking up windows and doorways, blockwork, brickwork and stud wall construction.

  • Damp proofing

    Anything from a patch of damp to a flooded basement: rising damp, penetrating damp, condensation, ventilation and basement waterproofing.
