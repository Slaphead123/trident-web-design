What the studio does for your dog

From a quick tidy to a full groom. This is the studio’s own list of services.

  • Nail clipping and cutting

    Overgrown nails can chip or break and even need a vet. The groomers keep them short and healthy.

  • Washing and de-matting

    Bathing, de-matting and ear cleaning, using what the studio calls some of the safest products on the market.

  • Cutting and blow dry

    A cut and blow dry to keep the coat in good order, from long coats to short.

  • Hand stripping

    Removing old loose fur so the new coat can grow in. Also offered for short-coated dogs, or if you would rather not have a trim.

  • Trimming, tidy ups and ear plucking

    General tidy ups and scissor cutting, for dogs and puppies.

Prices. The studio’s current website does not list any, so ask when you book.
