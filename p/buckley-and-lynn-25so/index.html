We’re committed to transforming lifestyles through practical and professionally designed building projects.

Buckley & Lynn Ltd were founded by Thomas Buckley and Rory Parkinson-Lynn. They have overseen the development of new builds, commercial and residential work, as well as bathrooms, kitchens and extensions, throughout Colchester and surrounding areas of Essex.

Planning, design and helpful advice come with the building, and health and safety is an integral part of the business, so the working environment is safe.
