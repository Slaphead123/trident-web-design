What I plaster

Every job starts with proper preparation, so the finished surface is smooth, even and ready for decorating. Internal work only.

  • Wall and ceiling skimming

    A smooth, even surface ready for painting or decorating. Right for refreshing tired or uneven plaster.

  • Plaster repairs

    Cracks, holes, damaged plaster and uneven areas made good before a new finish goes on, small or large.

  • Wall and ceiling re-skimming

    A fresh, smooth finish over the existing plaster, without taking it all off.

  • Skimming over Artex

    Suitable Artex and textured surfaces skimmed smooth. The surface is assessed first.

  • Ceiling repairs

    Cracks, damaged or worn ceilings repaired, or prepared for a complete re-skim.

  • Plasterboard and dry lining

    Walls, ceilings and partitions boarded, then plastered and finished where needed.

  • Small jobs

    One wall, a patch, a small ceiling repair. No job is too small.

Questions

Can you plaster over Artex?

In many cases, yes. Suitable Artex and textured surfaces can be plastered over for a smooth, modern finish. The type and condition of the surface need to be assessed first.

Do you do external plastering or rendering?

No. I specialise in internal plastering and do not carry out external plastering, rendering or other external wall work.

How long does plastering take?

It depends on the size and condition of the property and how much preparation is needed. Smaller jobs may take a day; several rooms can take several days. You get an estimated timescale with your quote.

How long does new plaster take to dry?

That depends on how thick it is, ventilation, temperature and humidity. It must dry thoroughly before painting, and I can tell you when it is ready for decorating.

What should I do before you arrive?

Where possible, move furniture and belongings away from the walls and ceilings being plastered. We can talk through any other preparation before work starts.

Do you give quotes?

Yes. Free, no-obligation estimates, with a clear price for the job.
