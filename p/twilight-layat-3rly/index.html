Slow, one-to-one, and never left alone.

An elderly cockerpoo who has rarely seen a grooming parlour needs more time than a two-year-old seen every six weeks. So there is no clock.

  • One dog at a time

    It is strictly 1-2-1. Your dog is never left unattended while other dogs are processed.

  • An arm's length away

    In the bath or on the table, a DBS checked member of staff is a literal arm's length from your dog at all times. No exceptions.

  • Door locked, screen pulled

    A privacy barrier is pulled across and the door is locked, so nobody can walk in and disturb the groom.

  • As long as your dog needs

    There are no strict deadlines. The groom takes as long as the dog says. WhatsApp updates are available whenever you ask.

Questions owners ask.

My dog is nervous and had a bad experience. How will you be different?

Everything is 1-2-1, with restraint-free and fear-free sessions. You can stay for the whole groom if you think it will help. Isabelle listens to you and to your dog. If they are afraid of the hairdryer, there are methods around that.

Will your dogs be there? Mine cannot be in the same room as another dog.

On request, no. Let them know in advance and the managers will work from home on the day of your appointment.

Can I drop in?

Sometimes, for small things such as nails, training advice or a chat. Keep an eye on Instagram, @twilightlayat, for those days.

What if my dog is matted?

Knots and mats are cut out if brushing becomes too painful for the dog. Prices often follow the size of the dog: a Bubble for a Chihuahua is £30, a cockerpoo £40 and a Labradoodle £50.

What does Layat' mean?

It means 'to bark' in Russian, a play on words from Isabelle's first business idea, Twilight Bark.

Good to know. Appointments can be changed free with 48 hours' notice. A no-show may carry a £15 fee. Not happy with the trim? Come back free within 7 days and it will be altered.
