Starting out

First time? Email first, then come in with your dog.

  1. Email with a few basics about your dog’s needs, to discuss how to register.

  2. Choose a date and time for a guided tour, a good chat and help with the paperwork.

  3. Bring your dog or dogs so they can check the day care is a great fit, with temperament monitoring.

  4. Book ahead as far as you like once registered. Pay online or on the day.
