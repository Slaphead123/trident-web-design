Come in and watch. The parlour is open.

Your dog gets one to one attention in a calm parlour, and the aim is to have them back with you in a reasonable time, which Four Paws agrees with you when you book. All staff are animal lovers and fully qualified, and Four Paws Pet Grooming Ltd is fully insured.

  • One to one attention
  • Not left unattended
  • Loved and respected
  • You are told about any problems
  • A free health check: gums, skin, ears, teeth
  • Full groom Cut, wash, dry, de-matt, comb, ears, eyes and nails.
  • Hand stripping Plucking out the old, dead outer coat so new wire hair comes through. They are specialists.
  • Clipping and styling Keeps the coat matt free and in good condition.
  • Bath and tidy Every six weeks is their advice, between full grooms. Not for coats left over three months.
  • Nails and glands Nail clipping, and emptying full anal glands for relief.
  • All breeds Small to large. The biggest dogs may not fit the hydrobath and are collected and delivered: call first.
