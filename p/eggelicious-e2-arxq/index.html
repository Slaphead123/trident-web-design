A Swindon staple since 2009.

The Eggelicious story began in September 2009 at the original Tented Market in Swindon town centre. We built our reputation on high-quality, delicious food and a personal touch, then opened E2 on Wood Street in Old Town in 2014.

Our E3 branch in the Brunel has closed permanently, a casualty of the COVID-19 pandemic. Wood Street is home, and we are still going strong.

Slow food fast

Hearty, wholesome meals, freshly prepared each day and cooked right before your eyes. Signature toasted wraps, plus box meals for anyone avoiding carbs.

Don’t let the name fool you. Eggs feature in our cooking but they don’t define it. Meat-eater, vegetarian or vegan, there is a wide choice every day, with free-range eggs.

Ash and family

Ash Mistry trained as a biochemist and never imagined frying eggs for a living. The recession changed that. Eggelicious is a family business: Ash, his wife Sheela and their three children.

Fresh coffee, hot chocolate and chai latte to go with your wrap. Hot drinks start at £2.00.
