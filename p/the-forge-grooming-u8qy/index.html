Why a dog’s price is an estimate.

Every dog is unique, and that is how each one is treated. Four things set the time and care needed.

Coat condition
A well maintained coat takes less time than one that is matted or tangled, which needs extra time, care, products and skill.
Behaviour
Nervous or anxious dogs may need extra handling time and breaks. Patience and gentle handling take time.
Style requested
A short trim takes far less brushing and drying than 2 to 3 inches of coat, and breed standard trims take more care than a one-length clip.
Breed and size
Mixed breeds vary widely in size, coat type and density.

For example: one Cockapoo may be small with a fine wavy coat and take 1.5 hours, while another is larger with a thick dense curl and takes 2.5 hours.

The Emmi-pet ultrasonic toothbrush is silent, non-vibrating and non-invasive, and cleans without anaesthesia. The ultrasound works up to 12mm beneath the gumline to kill bacteria and reduce plaque and tartar.
