Meet Vicky and Jamie

Vicky Taylor is co-owner and carries out every treatment. She is an Advanced Practitioner who specialises in facial contouring and anti-wrinkle treatments.

She started on her own aesthetics journey at 26, while she was a secondary school maths teacher, and moved her career across. Vicky and Jamie built the clinic into their own home, so clients are treated in a private, relaxed setting.

Vicky takes monthly masterclasses to keep her techniques advanced, and believes no two clients are the same, so every treatment is tweaked for the individual.
