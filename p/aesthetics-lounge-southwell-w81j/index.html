Your practitioner

She used to teach maths. Now she treats faces.

Vicky Taylor is co-owner of the clinic and carries out every treatment. She is an Advanced Practitioner, specialising in facial contouring and anti-wrinkle treatments.

She began her own aesthetics journey at 26, while working as a very successful secondary school maths teacher. She became certain her maths background and her growing passion would work together, and her path into the industry began.

She and her co-owner Jamie built the clinic into their own home, so you are treated somewhere private and relaxed. She believes no two clients are the same, and takes monthly masterclasses so her techniques stay advanced.

Vicky’s specialisms

Every treatment is adjusted to the individual, using techniques Vicky keeps advanced through monthly masterclasses.

  • Anti-wrinkle

    One of her two specialisms.

  • Facial contouring

    The other: shaping and sculpting the face to suit you, not a standard plan.

Dermal filler

Made of hyaluronic acid, the substance that keeps us plump and youthful when we are young. Re-introducing it plumps deep skin folds and sculpts the face for an overall lifting effect.

  • Lip filler

    Adds definition and volume to the lip border and body.

  • Chin filler

    Shapes and defines the chin and jowl area for a more balanced lower face and profile.

  • Cheek filler

    Adds contour and volume where it has been lost, smoothing lines for a more refreshed look.

Skin boosters

Nourish the skin and aid its natural rejuvenation.

  • Profhilo

    Often described as an internal moisturiser. Boosts hydration, elasticity and firmness, and unlike filler has no cross-linking agents.

    Minor downtime

  • Jalupro under eye

    Stimulates your own collagen and elastin to plump out hollowness under the eye.

    Amino acids, not filler

  • Sunekos under eye

    Only 40% of clients are eligible for tear trough filler, because filler can attract water and worsen puffiness. Sunekos stimulates your own collagen and elastin instead.

    Also neutralises dark circles

Fat dissolve

A non-surgical way to remove stubborn pockets of fat. Ideal if you exercise regularly and eat a healthy diet.

  • Aqualyx

    Breaks down cell membranes so the fat cell is destroyed permanently, then your lymphatic system clears it. Stomach, love handles, arms, chin, inner and outer thighs.

    Injections 3 to 4 weeks apart
