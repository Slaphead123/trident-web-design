The treatments

Real descriptions from the clinic’s own treatments page. Choose a group.

Dermal filler

Made of hyaluronic acid, the natural substance in us that keeps us plump and youthful when we are young. As we age, collagen breaks down and volume is lost. Re-introducing hyaluronic acid plumps deep skin folds and sculpts the face for an overall lifting effect.

  • Lip fillerAdds definition and volume to the lip border and body.
  • Chin fillerShapes and defines the chin and jowl area, lengthening the lower face for a more balanced profile.
  • Cheek fillerAdds contour and volume to the cheeks where it has been lost, smoothing lines for a more youthful, refreshed look.

Skin boosters

Skin boosters nourish the skin and aid its natural rejuvenation.

  • ProfhiloBoosts hydration, elasticity and firmness by stimulating collagen. Often described as an internal moisturiser, and unlike dermal filler it has no cross-linking agents.Minor downtime
  • Jalupro under eyeStimulates your body to make its own collagen and elastin, plumping out hollowness under the eye.Amino acids, not filler
  • Sunekos under eyeStimulates your own collagen and elastin to plump hollow under eyes naturally, and neutralises dark circles that have formed. Only 40% of clients are eligible for tear trough filler, which is why some prefer it.Also neutralises dark circles

Fat dissolve

A non-surgical way to minimise stubborn pockets of fat. Ideal if you exercise regularly and keep a healthy diet.

  • AqualyxBreaks down the cell membranes to liquify the fat cell, destroying it permanently, then your lymphatic system clears it. Areas: stomach, love handles, arms, chin, inner and outer thighs.A set of injections 3 to 4 weeks apart

Hollow under the eyes? Three routes.

Tear trough filler
Only 40% of clients are eligible, because filler can attract water and make under-eye puffiness worse.
Jalupro
Made of amino acids, not filler. It stimulates your body to make its own collagen and elastin, plumping out the hollowness.
Sunekos
Does the same, and also neutralises dark circles that have formed. This is why some clients prefer it to Jalupro.

Vicky can talk you through which suits you at your free consultation.

She used to teach maths. Now she treats faces.

Vicky Taylor is co-owner of Aesthetics Lounge and carries out every treatment. She is an Advanced Practitioner who specialises in facial contouring and anti-wrinkle treatments.

She began her own journey in aesthetics at the age of 26, while working as a very successful secondary school maths teacher. She became certain that her maths background and her growing passion for aesthetics would work together, and her path into the industry began.

Vicky cares deeply about every client. She takes monthly masterclasses so her techniques stay advanced, and believes no two clients are the same, so every treatment should be individually tweaked.
