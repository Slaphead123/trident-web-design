Treatments

Prices are not on the clinic’s current website. Ask at your free consultation.

  • Anti-wrinkle

    One of Vicky’s two specialisms

    Every treatment is adjusted to the individual, using techniques Vicky keeps advanced through monthly masterclasses.

  • Facial contouring

    The other specialism

    Shaping and sculpting the face, tailored to you rather than to a standard plan.

  • Dermal filler

    Hyaluronic acid

    Plumps deep skin folds and sculpts the face for an overall lifting effect.

  • Lip filler

    Definition and volume

    Adds definition and volume to the lip border and body.

  • Chin filler

    A more balanced profile

    Shapes and defines the chin and jowl area, creating a more balanced lower face.

  • Cheek filler

    Volume where it has been lost

    Adds contour and volume, smoothing lines for a more refreshed look.

  • Profhilo skin booster

    Minor downtime

    Often described as an internal moisturiser: it boosts hydration, elasticity and firmness, and unlike filler has no cross-linking agents.

  • Jalupro under eye

    Amino acids, not filler

    A skin booster that stimulates your own collagen and elastin production, plumping out hollowness under the eye.

  • Sunekos under eye

    Also neutralises dark circles

    Stimulates collagen and elastin to plump hollow under eyes. Only 40% of clients are eligible for tear trough filler, which is why some prefer Sunekos.

  • Aqualyx fat dissolve

    Injections 3 to 4 weeks apart

    A non-surgical way to remove stubborn pockets of fat from the stomach, love handles, arms, chin and inner and outer thighs. Ideal if you exercise regularly and eat a healthy diet.

Vicky and Jamie

Vicky Taylor is co-owner and carries out every treatment. She is an Advanced Practitioner who specialises in facial contouring and anti-wrinkle treatments.

She began her own journey with aesthetics at 26, while she was a very successful secondary school maths teacher. She became certain that her maths background and her growing passion for aesthetics would work together, and her path into the industry began.

Vicky and Jamie built the clinic into their own home, so clients are treated in a private, relaxed setting. She takes monthly masterclasses to keep her techniques advanced, and believes no two clients are the same.
