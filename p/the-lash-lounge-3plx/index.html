Patch tests

Tints, brow lamination and peels need a patch test at least 48 hours before your appointment. Lash extension clients can have one for reassurance, or sign a consent form instead.

Before a lash appointment

Arrive with clean, makeup-free lashes. You will be emailed a consultation form to fill in beforehand, and given aftercare advice on the day.

Age

We cannot tint brows or lashes for anyone under 16, and brow lamination is not offered to anyone under 16.
