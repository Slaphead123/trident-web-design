Servicing without losing your warranty.

Chris Cars says it can service and repair vehicles of all ages without affecting your warranty, because of ‘block exemption’ regulations brought in by the European Union in 2004.

Its price will be significantly lower than the main dealers’, it says, and it uses approved OE-specification (original equipment specification) parts.

2000

A small start

Chris Curry opened Chris Cars on 1 September 2000 in a unit on English Street Industrial Estate, and soon took on more staff.

2005

New home

The business outgrew two units and spent a hectic weekend moving to Edgar Street, ready to open on the Monday morning.

2013

Tidal surge

The surge of 5 December 2013 hit English Street badly. Fully insured, Chris was open again at the start of 2014 with new equipment.
