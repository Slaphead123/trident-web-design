Good to know before you come.

Matted coats

Up to 20 minutes of de-matting in a single session, usually by two groomers, with an additional charge of £15.

Vaccinations and fleas

Please make sure your dog is up to date with flea treatment and vaccinations. The salon does not apply flea treatment shampoos.

What the salon does not do

Expressing anal glands (a job for your vet) and hand stripping.

Shop

Natural shampoos, grooming brushes, combs, canine colognes and accessories such as neckerchiefs, collars and leads.
