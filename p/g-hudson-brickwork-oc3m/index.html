Repointing

Why repoint?

The mortar between the bricks of your home’s walls is a crucial part of the overall construction of your property. Over time, through weathering and decay, holes and gaps can appear in it. That doesn’t look good, and more importantly it can let in water. This can lead to damp problems and cause long term significant damage if not repaired.

Repointing your bricks is the most effective way of ensuring your walls are weatherproof, and it repairs the structural integrity of your property.

  • Stopping damp getting in
  • The physical appearance of your property
  • Adding value: first impressions count
  • Saleability: poor mortar is commonly raised as a problem on a valuer’s report

How Glenn repoints your brickwork

  1. 1

    Old mortar out

    Removed with angle grinders, giving clean cuts to the top and bottom joints, ready for the new mortar.

  2. 2

    Hand tools where needed

    Where power tools can’t be used, Glenn’s team uses hand tools such as comb hammers.

  3. 3

    Brickwork cleaned

    The brickwork is cleaned to give the new mortar maximum grip.

  4. 4

    New mortar applied

    Fresh mortar goes into the clean joints, and the wall is weatherproof again.
