Threading, tinting, waxing and facials.

Four treatments, all given by or under the guidance of Vibhuti Bhiker, a VTCT certified beauty therapist, in private, peaceful treatment rooms.

  • Threading

    The ancient art of removing hair from the roots with cotton thread. Regrowth is slower and finer, leaving the skin clean and smooth. Used on the whole face and the upper lip for a sharp, naturally polished line.

  • Tinting

    Eyebrow tint is a semi-permanent dye made for brows. The therapists make sure you leave with a well-shaped, arched brow.

  • Waxing

    Eyebrow waxing and full body waxing, using different types of wax. Intimate waxing too, including Brazilian and Hollywood.

  • Facials

    Skin changes over time, so the facials are sorted by what they do. Whether you want more hydration or a quick fix, the therapists will suggest the right one.
