Every full groom includes

  • Health check of your dog for any obvious concerns.
  • Groom through to remove knots, tangles, dead hair or shedding hair.
  • Inspection of foot pads for excess hair and debris.
  • Trimming nails.
  • Ear care: removing excess hair and cleaning.
  • Trimming of the sanitary area.
  • Bathing in a luxurious Hydrobath using a high quality shampoo specially formulated for the specific coat needs of your pet, including a conditioner if needed.
  • Drying using a professional stand dryer whilst brushing.
  • Styling in a breed specific haircut, or a style of your choice. Techniques offered include clipping, scissoring, hand-stripping.
  • Finishing fragrance spray.

Brush-out and de-matting

Every full groom comes with a 30 minute brush-out included. Matted fur does not allow air circulation to the skin. Over time, this can cause hot spots, bacterial and fungal infections. Fleas, ticks, and other parasites may be living in the matted coat, causing further skin problems.

£6 per 15 min for excessive matting

Puppy pampering

Three gentle visits

Your puppy should have a positive, enjoyable first experience of being groomed. Our suggested visit schedule is below, with free leaflets to take away on coat care at home.

  • Visit 1: meet the studio

    12 to 16 weeks of age, once fully vaccinated. About half an hour: meet the groomer, the sights, sounds and smells, gentle handling of face, ears and feet, gentle brushing, nails if needed.

    £5

  • Visit 2: bath and dry

    16 to 20 weeks, or about 2 to 4 weeks after visit 1. About 1 hour: bath and dry, ear and feet care, trimming around the eyes if the breed needs it.

    £10

  • Visit 3: first full groom

    About 2 to 4 weeks after visit 2. Some trimming of the coat if required, plus ear and feet care.

    Normal price, less £15

Ongoing visits

Your groomer will advise you on the recommended time between professional grooms. You can choose a full groom each visit, or a mini groom in between full grooms.

Grooming with a difference

All breeds are lovingly and expertly cared for as if they were our own.

Proprietor Sadie Gelsthorpe started her grooming career as an apprentice in an Exmouth salon in 2003. After 10 years in the industry, and an aim to exceed all expectations in quality of service, she now offers a caring, personal, top quality dog grooming service in her own clean, modern salon.

Whether your dog wants grooming, bathing, or just a day of pure indulgence, they will receive one to one attention from one of our qualified dog lovers. We welcome all breeds, all sizes, nervous and temperamental dogs.
