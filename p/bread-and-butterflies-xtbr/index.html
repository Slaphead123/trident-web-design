The cake counter is vegan too.

A full counter of cakes and bakes with no dairy or egg in any of them. A few from the tearoom:

  1. Fruit scones with coconut cream and jam
  2. Millionaire’s shortbread
  3. Chocolate tiffin cake
  4. Raspberry, yoghurt and coconut cake

“To name but a few.” Ask at the counter for what is in today, and tell the team about any allergies or intolerances.

A shelf of hand drawn gifts by the counter.

Cushions, mugs, candles, macramé and wire sculptures, to suit all budgets. There is no online shop, so pop in for a browse.

Have your pet drawn on a cushion: Linsey will also draw a favourite sports personality or singer. Lisa takes commissions for macramé plant hangers. Get in touch to talk it over.

Run by Linsey and Lisa.

After trialling a vegan month in January 2022, they decided to go 100% vegan, and were excited to see so many customers trying plant based alternatives and enjoying them.

The menu is colourful, tasty, healthy and cruelty free. Linsey and Lisa always appreciate feedback, and if you are ever dissatisfied, they would like you to come and have a chat.
