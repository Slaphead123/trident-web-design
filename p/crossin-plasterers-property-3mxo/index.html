What we do

We do Plastering, skimming, plastering over Artex, Artexing, coving and cornicing, replacing damaged ceilings, dry lining, DOT and DAB, screeding, dry wall tape and jointing, Knauf products, magnetic plastering, rendering, inside and out, colour rendering, pebble dashing, imitation limestone, eco (insulated) lime plastering, traditional lime plastering, polished or Venetian plastering, Marmorino, Stucco Lustro, Tadelakt Moroccan polished plastering, damp proofing, carpentry, tiling, kitchen and bathroom fitting, painting and decorating, flooring, garage conversions, extensions, house remodelling, property servicing, and general renovation.
