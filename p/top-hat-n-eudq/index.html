Matted coats

Dogs with more than moderate matting need extra attention. The Animal Welfare Act 2006 limits the time spent brushing out a matted area to 15 minutes. If the mats will not come out, they are shaved off, leaving as much coat as possible. The extra charge depends on how severe the matting is and the time taken.

Take your time

Some dogs need a little extra time, especially if they are unsure about nail clipping or de-matting. Others need a little encouragement to relax and enjoy their grooming. Dog treats often help.

Not offered

Anal glands are not cleared, as this should be carried out by your vet. Teeth are not cleaned either.
