Come for the cuppa and catch-up as much as the hair.

Founded by managing director Rachael Grace-Leyland in 2009, The Strand started out as a hair and beauty salon in the heart of Derby city centre, and is well known for its expert hair and beauty services as well as its traditional barbershop.

We pride ourselves on a warm, welcoming and friendly environment every time you visit. Our stylists and therapists only use the best quality products for our services and treatments, and as a milk_shake premier salon we are the first to know about new products and techniques.

If you are looking for somewhere to relax, unwind and be pampered, get in touch and let Rachael and the team show you how it is done.
