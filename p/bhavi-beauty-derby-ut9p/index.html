What the salon offers

  • Skincare

    Facials that start with a detailed consultation and a skin analysis, using technology that picks up skin conditions you cannot see. Tailored to you, with cleansing, exfoliation, steam and extraction where needed, then a massage and mask.

  • Waxing

    Stay smoother for longer. Strip wax or hot wax, from brows to full body, with results that last up to 4 to 6 weeks.

  • Massage and bodycare

    Treatments to relax, replenish and revive mind and body, with ESPA.

  • Nails

    Dry, brittle nails? Nails for an occasion? Shellac, Gelish and OPI to suit your style.

  • Super Hair Removal

    Their pain free alternative to laser and IPL, for hair removal and skin rejuvenation.

  • Threading

    The Indian art of removing hair from the root with a coiled thread. Used for brows, lips and the full face.
