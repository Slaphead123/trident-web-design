Broken or slipped tiles

A tile usually breaks from the impact of a branch, or from a hairline crack nobody saw when the roof was laid. Replace it sooner rather than later, before the damage reaches the roof structure.

Slipped or cracked slates

Slate is hard-wearing, but it can outlive its fixings. A slipped, cracked or loose slate is usually visible from the ground, and fixing it early prevents an internal leak.

Valleys, verges and lead

Roof valleys and verges are common weak spots over time and can mean significant water damage if left. Lead work and chimney re-pointing are part of the same job list.

Blocked or failing gutters

Overflow, clogging, sag, leaks and gaps are the usual gutter problems. Watertight clears, repairs and replaces guttering, and fits fascias and soffits.

Perished flat roofs

Flat roofs fail from an impact leak or from one badly made or worn area. Whole roofs are renewed in torch-on felt or fibreglass GRP.
