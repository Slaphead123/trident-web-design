Less mess in your kitchen

We take the panels out of your oven and clean them in a van-mounted dip tank, so the grease stays in the van, not your kitchen. We take the oven door apart to clean between the layers of glass. A lightbulb change and filter change are included if the oven needs it.

  • Non caustic
  • Safe for children and pets

Cleaning for homes, lets and offices.

Tell us what you need and where you are, and Keen 2 Clean will come back to you about it.

  • Weekly or fortnightly

    Busy lifestyles, covered. We meet you and your home first, then devise a unique rota: the essentials every visit and a few larger jobs rotated in. Be home, or make us keyholders. No contract, so you can try us.

  • One off cleaning

    A family occasion, pre-Christmas or spring cleaning: a deeper, top to bottom clean of every room. We can also do washing machines, fridge freezers, ovens, windows, under beds, front and back doors and grout.

  • End of tenancy

    Help getting your deposit back, with a professional cleaning invoice to show your landlord. Can include carpet cleaning and mould treatment, and a bigger team when the timescale is tight.

  • Landlords

    We manage the portfolios of many professional landlords around Coventry, often cleaning weekly on their behalf, so damage and tenant concerns get reported and there are no nasty surprises at the end of the term.

  • Letting agents

    Experienced with letting agents’ accounts, and able to provide cleaners at short notice. Contact us to discuss commission fees.

  • Students

    No need to spend your last days at university cleaning. We arrange an end of tenancy clean for you.

  • Council cleans

    We have worked with Social Services and are experienced at restoring a house to a rentable condition or working with vulnerable clients.

  • Offices

    Outsource your cleaning without any employer liability. We are insured to work in your offices, and send monthly or weekly invoices.

  • After builders cleans

    Builders’ dust and grouting dust cleared and your home made ready for living in, for homeowners and property companies on tight exchange-to-completion schedules.
