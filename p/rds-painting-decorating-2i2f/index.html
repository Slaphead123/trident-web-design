Why buy a whole new kitchen when we can give your old one a new lease of life?

Many people look at their kitchen and think it needs replacing, when all it needs is a professional kitchen specialist to bring it back to its former glory. RDS are Southampton, Winchester and the New Forest's specialists in traditional hand-painted kitchens. Get that new-kitchen feeling at a fraction of the cost of replacing your kitchen.

Preparation. The team removes and cleans all handles, sanitises and de-greases doors and drawers, and masks worktops with lining paper and tape, then lays a protective spill-proof, impact-resistant board. Sanding. Festool dust-free sanding equipment is used on every part of the kitchen, ready for primer and top coats. Extra time here stops chipping and peeling. Priming. A high-adhesion primer, Benjamin Moore Stix or Caparol Haft, which can be tinted to match your chosen top coat for better coverage and depth of colour. Top coats. At least two top coats of your chosen colour. Benjamin Moore Advance comes in matt, satin and gloss finishes.

What RDS do

RDS do interior decorating, from specialist or practical wallpapering to advice on a colour scheme that suits you; exterior painting and restoration, expertly sanding and repainting timber windows or applying high-performance, weatherproof coatings to external walls; woodwork restoration for tired banisters, marked doors and worn skirting boards, in Benjamin Moore paints; dust-free sanding with Festool extraction, because the best finishes start with the best preparation; hand-painted kitchens and furniture; commercial decorating for local businesses, schools, colleges, nursing homes, home builders, estate agents and supermarkets, including Sainsbury's, Little Brook Retirement Home, Whitewoods Letting Agencies, Leaders Letting Agencies and Pearsons Estate Agencies, with minimum possible disruption; and English Heritage and listed building restoration, with an understanding of the materials and the strict guidelines of the process.

RDS recommend Benjamin Moore for exceptional quality and commitment to sustainability, and also offer Mylands of London, Farrow & Ball, Little Greene and Fired Earth. For everyday jobs they use quality paints from Dulux, Crown, Johnstone's and Armstead Trade.
