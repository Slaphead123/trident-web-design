Six kinds of clean.

Squeaky Cleaners clean homes and offices around Nottingham. They quote each job rather than list prices, so ask for yours below.

  • House cleaning

    Regular home cleaning on a schedule that suits you, for the whole house or chosen rooms. Say how you like things done and they follow it.

  • End of tenancy

    For tenants getting a deposit back and landlords filling an empty property. Can include rubbish disposal, carpet and upholstery cleaning, and a clear out of storage units and appliances.

  • Deep cleaning

    Skirting boards, door frames, light switches, door handles and behind appliances, with small brushes for the corners. Bathrooms and kitchens get the most attention.

  • Office cleaning

    A walk round your office first, then hours that keep disruption low for staff. Carpet and entrance rug cleaning is the most common add-on.

  • Airbnb cleaning

    A full clean between guests with linen and towels changed and surfaces sanitised, so the next review starts well.

  • Carpet and rug cleaning

    Proper equipment to lift dirt, dust and allergens from carpets and rugs, and to help them last.

How a quote works.

  1. 1

    Tell them what you need

    Send the form with your address and a few photos, and they can give a provisional quote quickly. A reply is promised within 24 hours.

  2. 2

    A visit, if you want one

    They would rather meet you face to face to go through what you actually want, for a home or an office.

  3. 3

    A checklist everyone agrees

    Every area and item is written down, split by kitchen, bathroom, bedrooms and living areas if needed, so nothing is missed.

  4. 4

    The quote, then the clean

    Work is typically priced on an hourly rate that depends on how often, where, and what is included. No obligation.
