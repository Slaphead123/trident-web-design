Cornices in London

Decorative cornices can create a traditional and beautiful look. Our plaster moulded cornices are available in all shapes and sizes and are made to fit your home or public building, such as a library or university.

Fire surrounds, door surrounds and corbels

Make your fireplace the centrepiece of your room with a beautifully made fire surround. From simple designs to fully embellished surrounds, we ensure that each one is created with precision and care.

To have any of these made for you, call 0208 449 8616 or 07973 624 706

Plaster moulded columns

Columns add a real sense of opulence and importance to whichever building or room they are being applied to. We create beautiful columns direct for you and install them in your home or public building, using traditional methods.

To enquire about a column, call 0208 449 8616 or 07973 624 706

Ceiling centres

Our plaster ceiling mouldings, also known as ceiling roses, can be used in any room and come in a variety of circumference sizes. From understated and simple ceiling centres to more extravagant and ornate ceiling roses, we supply and install a wide variety.

To enquire about a ceiling centre, call 0208 449 8616 or 07973 624 706

Panel moulds and dado rails

We use traditional panel moulding techniques to create panel moulds for your home or public building. They work well with other plaster mouldings such as cornices, fire surrounds and columns.

To enquire about a panel mould, call 0208 449 8616 or 07973 624 706
